MusicXML (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) gets the full play-along: a cursor follows you, the keys to press light up in rose, and in Wait for me the music only moves on when you hit the right notes.

PDF or images open as pages to read from while you play. Turn pages with the buttons, PageUp/PageDown, or your soft (left) pedal.

Tip: export MusicXML from MuseScore, or convert a PDF with an OMR tool such as Audiveris or PlayScore. You can also drop a file here.
